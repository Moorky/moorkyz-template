Write a design during **Researching and Planning** when a change is hard to
reverse or affects several areas: a new tool or command, a public interface,
data handling, a security boundary or cross-cutting behavior. Small, local
fixes do not need one. Record the lasting choices it settles with the
[decision template](DECISION_TEMPLATE.md) and the delivered evidence in an
[audit](AUDIT_TEMPLATE.md).
